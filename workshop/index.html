Meet Manika Goel
Mind Performance Coach & Counselling Psychologist

With my own son, I'd make him reread his lessons again and again — sometimes twenty times over — and it still wouldn't stay with him. But he wasn't confused. He could answer my questions and explain the concept back to me easily. That's when it hit me: this was never about his intelligence. Nobody — including me — had ever taught him how to actually remember what he studied.

Since then, as a Mind Performance Coach and Counselling Psychologist, I've sat across from parent after parent saying some version of the same thing: "I know my child is capable — so why don't the marks show it?" Almost every time, the missing piece wasn't willpower or discipline. It was focus, memory, and how the brain actually learns — the exact things mind training builds, and marks alone can never measure.
