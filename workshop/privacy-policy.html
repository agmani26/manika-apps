Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page explains what information we collect when you register for the Freedom From Distraction workshop, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and simple.

What we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and email address, which you type into the registration form.
  • Basic visit data such as your device type and which pages you viewed, collected through the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and similar tools.

How we use it

  • To confirm your seat and send you the workshop joining link and reminders on WhatsApp and email.
  • To share the bonuses and follow-up information related to the workshop.
  • To understand how well our pages and ads work, so we can improve them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your details. We only use trusted service providers needed to run the workshop, such as our website host, our payment partner (Razorpay), messaging provider, video meeting tool and Meta, for ads measurement. They may only use your information to provide their service.

Messages you receive

By registering, you agree to receive messages about this workshop and related programs from us. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying "STOP" or by leaving the WhatsApp group or community.

Your child's information

This page is for parents. We do not ask children to submit any information on this page.

Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online system is completely risk free, but we never spam and we do not publish your details.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you gave us. Message us on Instagram at @beingperformer and we will help.

Disclaimer

This site is not part of the Facebook or Instagram websites or of Meta Platforms, Inc., and is not endorsed by Meta in any way. The workshop shares educational information on focus and memory. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Results vary from child to child and are not guaranteed.
