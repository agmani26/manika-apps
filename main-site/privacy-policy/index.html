Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Performers' Club ("we", "us", "our"), a proprietorship of Manika Goel operating under the brand Being Performer and the website manikagoel.com, collects, uses and protects your personal information. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, city, and your child's name, age and class when you register for a workshop, training, program or session, or contact us.
  • Information shared during services: notes, assessments and DMIT (dermatoglyphics) fingerprint data shared for counselling and coaching purposes.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by third-party gateways such as Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: browser, device, IP address and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools such as analytics and the Meta (Facebook) pixel.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the programs, workshops, sessions and reports you sign up for.
  • To send you joining links, reminders, receipts and important updates, by email, WhatsApp, SMS or phone.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To share relevant information about our programs. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • To improve our website and measure our advertising.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

3. Children's information

Our services are meant for parents and guardians of children aged 8 to 18. We collect information about a child only from, or with the consent of, the parent or guardian. We use it only to provide the service and never to advertise to the child or to build behavioural profiles for advertising.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run the business (payment gateways, scheduling tools such as Topmate, email/WhatsApp messaging tools, video and hosting platforms, analytics and advertising platforms), and only as needed for them to do their job. We may also disclose information if required by law or a government authority.

5. Cookies and advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. We may use the Meta pixel and similar tools to measure and improve our ads. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work properly if you do.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw your consent, or stop marketing messages, by writing to info@performersclub.in. We will respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Third-party links

Our website links to other sites (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Topmate and others). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, write to info@performersclub.in.
